Experience across projects,
businesses and public service.

Independent advice led by David Chandler AM, an industry practitioner with wide experience in the private and public sector in housing, urban renewal, health and major projects including as Construction Director for Australia’s New Parliament House.

David’s experiences have evolved from projects and businesses operating around the Asia Pacific Rim. He was the inaugural NSW Building Commissioner and the industry founder of the Centre for Smart Modern Construction at Western Sydney University.

He brings essential perspectives on quality, productivity and better housing delivery and construction enterprise business strategies.

He champions the theme of ‘what good looks like’ to help shape the capability and character of today’s constructors, who are now facing into a rapidly modernising industry.
